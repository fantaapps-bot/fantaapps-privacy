Taiwan Campsites — Privacy Policy

FanTaApps · 2026-11-25 · 繁體中文

No account is required. Taiwan Campsites does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or storage permissions. The campsite list, filters and search work on your device using data bundled with the app. The current version does not send your searches, selections or favorites to the developer.

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to show ads and privacy choices. Google may collect or share IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where available, you can revisit your choices through Privacy options in the app.

The map uses the Google Maps SDK, and some buttons open Google Maps or the campsite's website in your browser. Those services are provided by their owners and are subject to their own privacy policies. Campsite information comes from public sources and may be out of date. The camper score is a reference value only.
