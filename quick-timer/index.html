Quick Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Quick Timer is a free app from Fanta Apps. The app does not require an account. Timer settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to Fanta Apps.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Quick Timer uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. Google encrypts SDK data in transit. Google's use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

UMP presents consent choices where required and can provide a privacy-options form. Ads are requested only when the SDK reports that they may be requested. Your choices may affect ad personalization and availability.

Permissions and local data

Notifications and exact-alarm access are used to alert you when a countdown ends. These permissions and timer settings are handled on your device. Quick Timer does not use its own analytics service or transmit your timer entries.

Children

Quick Timer is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. Questions can be sent to fantaapps@gmail.com.
