隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 3 月 14 日

概述

「日期鬧鐘」（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）由 Fanta Apps 開發。本隱私權政策說明我們如何處理您的資訊。

資料收集

本應用程式不會收集、儲存或傳輸任何個人資料。您建立的所有事件資料（名稱、日期、備註等）僅儲存在您的裝置本機，不會上傳至任何伺服器。

廣告

本應用程式使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google 可能會根據其自身的隱私權政策收集某些資訊（如廣告 ID、裝置資訊），以提供個人化廣告。詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

權限

  • 通知權限：用於發送鬧鐘提醒通知。
  • 精確鬧鐘權限：用於在指定時間觸發鬧鐘。
  • 開機啟動：用於在裝置重新啟動後恢復已設定的鬧鐘。

資料安全

由於所有資料僅儲存在您的裝置上，您的資料安全取決於您裝置本身的安全性。解除安裝本應用程式將永久刪除所有儲存的事件資料。

兒童隱私

本應用程式不針對 13 歲以下的兒童，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資訊。

政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將在此頁面上發布。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Overview

"Date Alarm" (the "App") is developed by Fanta Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

This App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All event data you create (names, dates, notes, etc.) is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded to any server.

Advertising

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect certain information (such as advertising ID and device information) according to its own privacy policy to serve personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Permissions

  • Notification Permission: Used to send alarm reminder notifications.
  • Exact Alarm Permission: Used to trigger alarms at specified times.
  • Boot Receiver: Used to restore scheduled alarms after device restart.

Data Security

Since all data is stored only on your device, your data security depends on your device's own security. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all stored event data.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
