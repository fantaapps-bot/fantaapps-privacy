Funny Wheel Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Funny Wheel is a free app from Fanta Apps. The app does not require an account. Your wheel options are stored locally on your device and are not sent to Fanta Apps. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Funny Wheel uses Google Mobile Ads to show banner ads and rewarded ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, ad interactions, app usage and diagnostic information such as app and SDK startup time and crash or responsiveness data, and device or advertising identifiers, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The app does not request precise location permission and does not read GPS coordinates. Google encrypts SDK data in transit. Google's use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

UMP presents consent choices where required and can provide a privacy-options form. Ads are requested only when the SDK reports that they may be requested. Your choices may affect ad personalization and availability.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads. It does not use contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location permissions.

Children

Funny Wheel is a general-purpose app and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. Questions can be sent to fantaapps@gmail.com.
